## Why this is its own channel (not press)

Press gets a demo and a key. Creators get something they can hold up on camera:
**a workflow template built for their exact stack.** A YouTuber who covers
Raycast or Keyboard Maestro doesn't want a press release — they want a thing to
show. The offer is the artifact, not the ask.

The collab is unpaid and there is no coverage expectation. If Helm earns a
mention, good. If it doesn't, they keep the key and the workflow. Say that
plainly — it's the honest version and it reads better than a transactional ask.

## Who this is for — wave 1 targets

Micro-creators, **5K–50K** subscribers/followers, whose audience already pays
for Mac utilities. Big enough to matter, small enough that a personal message
from the maker is a novelty, not noise.

**Fit signals (need at least two):**
- Covers Raycast, Alfred, Keyboard Maestro, Warp, or menu-bar utilities
- "My Mac setup" / "apps I use" / productivity-workflow content
- macOS-first audience (not cross-platform Windows-heavy)
- Replies to comments — a creator who engages will read a cold email

**Explicitly not a fit:** general tech reviewers (phones/laptops), Notion/Figma
"aesthetic desk" channels with no utility audience, anyone Windows-first. Helm
is macOS only — a pitch to a Windows audience is wasted and reads as spam.

**How to build the list (founder, before Jun 20):** start from the comment
sections and "creators who also covered this" of recent Raycast / Alfred /
Keyboard Maestro / Warp videos; check who the Mac-utility X accounts retweet.
Aim for **5–6 best-fit creators for wave 1**, not a blast. Quality of match
beats list size — one creator whose audience is exactly right outperforms ten
who half-fit.

## The offer — custom workflow template + Pro key

Three ready-made templates, matched to the creator's audience, are the starting
point (tracked in `AD_TODO.md`):

- `ads/workflow_designer.json` — Figma, Linear, Chrome (design refs), Slack
- `ads/workflow_dev.json` — VS Code, Terminal, Chrome (docs), Slack, Notion
- `ads/workflow_founder.json` — Notion, Slack, Chrome (analytics), Linear, Superhuman

Pick the closest one, then adjust two or three apps to match what the creator
actually shows on their channel — that small personalization is the whole point.
For a creator worth the effort, offer to build one live on a short call.

**License mechanics:** keys are minted per-email via `scripts/generate-key.js`
and each activates on two machines — so the give is: one Pro key per creator,
sent to the address they reply from. Mint on reply, not in the first message
(a key in a cold email is easy to ignore; a key after a "yes" lands).
