## Why this channel

Every channel run so far (r/macapps, Show HN, indie X) reaches Mac enthusiasts
and builders — people who browse tools for enjoyment. Helm's buyer is an
executive or operator with a context-switching problem and a corporate card.
That person is on LinkedIn and nowhere else in the current mix. This is the
audience gap the growth plan exists to close.

Executive assistants and chiefs of staff are the force multiplier: they
configure and gatekeep the tools their principals use. One EA who adopts Helm
can put it in front of an executive who would never install a menu bar utility
on their own.

## Posting rules (LinkedIn-specific)

- **No external link in the post body.** LinkedIn suppresses reach on posts
  that send people off-platform. Put `get-helm.app` in the **first comment**,
  posted immediately after publishing, and say "link in the comments" in the
  body only when the post is actually a CTA post.
- **Native video, uploaded directly.** Use `marketing/helm-demo-20s.mp4`.
  LinkedIn video autoplays muted in-feed, so the first two seconds have to read
  without sound — lead on the click and the stack opening, not on a title card.
- **Post as a person, not an ad.** First person, a problem you had, what you
  did about it. The product is the last third of the post, never the first line.
- **Cadence: 2–3 posts per week.** Consistency beats volume. Below is three
  weeks of drafts — roughly eight posts — which is the runway to see whether
  the framing lands before committing to Tier 4 paid.
- **Best window:** weekday mornings, 7–9am in the audience's timezone. Helm's
  ICP is US-heavy, so this is late evening NZT. Schedule rather than post live.
- **Reply to every comment** for the first two hours. Comment replies are the
  strongest reach signal LinkedIn gives a post.
- **Hook discipline:** only the first ~2 lines show before "see more." The hook
  must stand alone and must not be a question that reads as bait.

## Comment engagement — the other half of the channel

Posting alone will not reach a cold audience. Half the value here is showing up
in other people's comment sections, where their audience already is.

- **Who to follow and reply to:** productivity writers, chief-of-staff and EA
  voices, operations leaders, "how I work" posters. Build a list of 15–20 and
  check them daily.
- **How to reply:** add a specific observation from your own experience. No
  product mention, no link. The goal is that people click your name.
- **Your profile is the landing page** for everyone who does click. Headline
  and featured section should say what Helm is and link to `get-helm.app`, so
  the comments never have to.
- **One rule:** never reply with the product. If someone else raises the
  problem, respond to the problem. If they ask what you use, then answer.

## EA / chief-of-staff community outreach

The growth plan calls this out as the force-multiplier segment. Value-first,
one message, no follow-up beyond a single nudge.

**Targets:** r/ExecutiveAssistants, EA and chief-of-staff Slack and Discord
groups, LinkedIn EA groups, operator newsletters. Read each group's self-promo
rules before posting — most require value-first participation and will remove a
cold pitch, which costs the account.

**Free-license offer for honest reviews:**
Mint per email with `node scripts/generate-key.js <email>` (2 machines per key).
No strings, no required coverage, no embargo. If the honest review is "this
doesn't fit my workflow," that's the outcome, and it's useful.

## ⚠️ Blocker — attribution is not wired up

The growth plan's step 4 ("add UTM tags to every download link") does not work
as written, for three reasons:

1. **The site has no analytics at all.** UTM parameters are inert unless
   something reads them. There is no analytics script on `website/index.html`.
2. **The privacy policy forbids the obvious fix.** `website/privacy.html` lists
   "Any usage analytics or behavioral data" under *Information We Do Not
   Collect*. In context that paragraph is about the app, not the website, but a
   reader will not make that distinction — and "nothing phones home" is load
   bearing in Helm's positioning. Adding a client-side analytics script
   contradicts published copy and should not be done silently.
3. **Download clicks bypass the site anyway.** `website/sections.jsx:5` points
   every download button straight at the R2 object
   (`pub-…r2.dev/Helm-1.0.3-universal.dmg`). Those clicks never touch a page you
   could instrument, and R2's metrics do not break down by referrer.

**Recommended fix — a redirect endpoint, not a tracker.** Serve
`get-helm.app/download?src=linkedin` as a Cloudflare Pages Function that counts
the `src` value server-side and 302s to the current DMG. Aggregate counts per
source, no cookies, no client script, no per-user data — which is compatible
with the privacy policy as written and does not weaken the privacy claim. It
also fixes a live latent bug: the download URL is pinned to `1.0.3` in the
source, so the next release ships without the site following it.

Separately, `website/privacy.html` is dated June 3 and still says waitlist
emails are deleted "once Helm has launched and all signups have been notified."
Helm launched July 1. That is a commitment with a date attached and it should
either be honoured or the wording changed.
