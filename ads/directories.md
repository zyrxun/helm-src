## Positioning boilerplate — "why not Raycast/Alfred/Bunch?"

Use this verbatim when a listing or comment asks for differentiation:

> Helm isn't a launcher. Launchers open one thing fast; Helm restores an
> entire working state — every app, every tab, every window, per Chrome
> profile — and tears it down again afterward. You don't write a config or a
> script: Capture reads your current screen and turns it into the workflow.

Listed alternatives to claim: **Bunch** (closest concept, config-file based),
**Workspaces by Apptorium**, **Keyboard Maestro** (adjacent, script-based),
**Raycast / Alfred** (adjacent, launchers).
