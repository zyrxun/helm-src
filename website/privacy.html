Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

Helm ("we", "our", or "us") is a macOS application built by an independent developer. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Information We Collect

Email address. When you join the waitlist or purchase a license, we collect your email address. This is used to send you a confirmation, deliver your license key, and notify you of the launch or major updates.

Payment information. Purchases are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card number. Stripe's privacy policy governs payment data: stripe.com/privacy.

Bug reports. Helm does not run any automatic crash or telemetry reporting. If you choose to use the in-app "Report a bug" feature, only the text you write (and, if you tick the box, the last lines of the app log) are sent to us by email. Nothing is sent unless you submit a report.

Download counts. This website has no analytics script, no cookies, and no third-party trackers. When you click a download button, we add 1 to a running total for that day and that referral channel — for example, "11 downloads from LinkedIn on August 11." That total is the entire record. We do not store your IP address, your browser, a visitor ID, or anything that could connect a download back to you.

Information We Do Not Collect

The Helm app does not collect, transmit, or store:

— The names, URLs, or contents of the apps and files in your workflows
— Your browsing history or tab URLs
— Keystrokes or clipboard contents
— Any usage analytics or behavioral data

Your workflows are stored locally on your Mac at ~/Library/Application Support/Helm/ and never leave your device.

How We Use Your Information

Your email is used only to:

— Send your license key after purchase
— Notify you when Helm ships (waitlist)
— Send critical security or update notices

We do not sell, rent, or share your email with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Retention

Waitlist emails are deleted once Helm has launched and all signups have been notified. License emails are retained as long as necessary to support your purchase.

Your Rights

You can request deletion of your data at any time by emailing hello@get-helm.app. We will remove your information within 30 days.

Third-Party Services

Helm uses the following third-party services:

— Stripe — payment processing (stripe.com/privacy)
— Resend — transactional email (resend.com/privacy)
— Cloudflare — website hosting and app downloads (cloudflare.com/privacypolicy)

Children

Helm is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top and notify active users by email.

Contact

Questions? Email us at hello@get-helm.app.
