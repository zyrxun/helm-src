Take the wheel.
↑
Helm lives in your menu bar
Look for the ship's wheel icon at the top right of your screen. Click it any time to open your workflows.
⌘
Can't see it?
Hold ⌘ Command and drag the wheel icon to reposition it in your menu bar.
🔐
Grant Accessibility access
Helm needs permission to open your apps. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility and enable Helm.
⊠
Launch or Teardown
Switch between Launch and Teardown at the bottom of the popover. In Teardown mode, your hotkeys and run buttons quit your stack instead of opening it — same key, opposite direction.
◐
Focus modes need a Shortcut
To attach a macOS Focus to a workflow, open the Shortcuts app and create two shortcuts named Enable [Mode] and Disable [Mode] — for example Enable Deep Work and Disable Deep Work. Each shortcut uses the Set Focus action to turn the matching focus on or off. Helm runs them automatically on launch and teardown. Open Shortcuts app
